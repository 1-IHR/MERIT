Multi-Perspective Equitable Routing for ICU Triage

ICU triage decisions are made under time pressure and limited information. A patient's care pathway, their capacity to communicate distress, the proportionality of intensive care given their condition . These are factors that often do not surface in the clinical presentation alone.

This study evaluates a decision support tool designed to bring those dimensions to the clinician's attention before the triage decision is made, with the goal of ensuring every patient receives a fair and fully informed assessment.

What you will see
Each case presents two stages:

Stage 1 (Clinical Information): the information available to the triage clinician at the time of the decision: chief complaint, vitals, labs, and medical history.

Stage 2 (Multi-Perspective Clinical Summary): a structured summary produced by three independent specialist reviewers assessing the same case from clinical, ethical, and equity perspectives. It does not make a recommendation. It surfaces considerations the clinical presentation alone may not carry, supporting a more complete and equitable assessment of each patient's needs.

Please answer the questions after Stage 1 before reading Stage 2.

There are no right or wrong answers. Your honest clinical judgment is what matters. Each case takes approximately 2 to 3 minutes.

These are de-identified clinical case summaries for research annotation purposes. No personally identifiable patient information is included.

All responses are stored securely and used solely for research purposes.
